This isn't just about low payouts; it's about a fundamental mismatch between human creativity and machine-driven discovery.

### The Algorithmic Trap: What's Really Happening?

Streaming platforms rely on complex algorithms designed to maximize user retention and platform revenue. This creates several inherent problems for artists:

*   **The Popularity Loop:** Algorithms favor what is already popular, creating a feedback loop where established hits and heavily promoted tracks get amplified, while new, independent music struggles for visibility. This is the antithesis of **activity over popularity**, where consistent creation should be rewarded.
*   **Homogenization of Sound:** To please the algorithm, there's pressure to create music that fits specific, data-proven patterns. This stifles innovation and punishes artistic risk-taking.
*   **Bots Don't Matter Here:** On major platforms, visibility can be gamed. Fake streams and inflated follower counts distort the landscape, making it impossible for authentic artists to compete fairly. At Sonora.Chat, our mantra is clear: **bots don't matter here**. Visibility is earned through real fan activity.

### The Human-Centered Alternative: Principles Over Algorithms

So, what's the solution? We believe it's a return to human-centric values. [Sonora.Chat](https://artists.sonora.chat) is built on core principles that directly address the failures of algorithmic systems:

1.  **Fans Are The True Promoters.** Instead of begging an algorithm for attention, artists on Sonora connect directly with listeners. Fans who believe in the music can "Hype" an artist, directly improving their search ranking. Discovery is community-driven, not corporation-driven.
2.  **Zero Cost to Publish or Be Heard.** There are no pay-to-play schemes. Artists upload their music for free, and listeners access it for free. Removing this financial barrier is the first step in dismantling the inequitable system.
3.  **Direct Fan Support, No Middlemen.** When a fan wants to support an artist, they can do so via integrated links (like Ko-fi) with 0% commission taken by Sonora. This aligns with our belief in **community-funded, not VC-funded** growth.
