## The Problem with "Viral or Bust"

The modern streaming economy is designed to reward sudden spikes in attention. Algorithms favor what's already trending, creating a cycle where artists feel pressured to game the system or pray for a lucky break. This "viral or bust" mentality sidelines the steady work of creation, the daily practice, and the gradual building of a real audience. It prioritizes metrics over music and popularity over passion.

## The Sonora.Chat Principle: Activity Over Popularity

Our first and foremost mantra is **Activity over popularity**. This isn't just a slogan; it's our operational system. On Sonora.Chat, visibility and discovery are tied to real, human engagement and consistent output. When you upload new music, interact with listeners, or receive "Hypes" from fans who believe in your work, you improve your presence. The platform rewards doers, not algorithms. This creates a healthier ecosystem where your next release is just as important as your last.

This principle is deeply connected to two others: **Bots don't matter here** and **Fans are the true promoters**. There are no fake streams or paid plays to artificially inflate your numbers. Instead, your growth is driven by authentic fan support.
