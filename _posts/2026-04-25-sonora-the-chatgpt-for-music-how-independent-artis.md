## What Is Sonora, the ChatGPT for Music?

Sonora is not your typical streaming service. It's an interactive chatbot that functions as a personalized music companion. You can talk to it, ask for song recommendations based on your mood or genre, and discover artists you've never heard before—all without ads, listening limits, or hidden fees. Think of it as ChatGPT for music, but with a soul: it's powered by real human expression, not AI-generated tracks.

### How It Works for Listeners

When you visit [Sonora.Chat](https://sonora.chat), you're greeted by a chatbot that understands your musical cravings. Type "I need something chill for a rainy afternoon" or "Show me indie rock from 2025," and Sonora searches the YouTube API to find the perfect mix. It also provides links to artist profiles, so you can dive deeper into their work. And because it's a Progressive Web App (PWA), you can [install Sonora.Chat](https://sonora.chat/installsonora.php) on your phone or desktop for instant access.

## Why Sonora.Chat Exists: A Rebellion Against the Pay-to-Play Economy

As an independent musician who codes, I've seen firsthand how big streaming platforms exploit artists. You pay to upload, pay to promote, and still get buried by viral hits and fake streams. That's why Sonora.Chat is built on eight core principles that prioritize human creativity over corporate profits.

### Core Principle: Activity Over Popularity

On Sonora.Chat, consistent work matters more than viral moments. The platform rewards artists who keep releasing music and engaging with fans, not those who game the algorithm. This means a bedroom producer with a monthly EP can rank higher than a label-backed act with millions of Instagram followers.

### Core Principle: Zero Cost to Publish or Be Heard

It's completely free for artists to upload music and get listeners. No pay-to-play, no hidden fees. Period. This aligns with our belief that music should be accessible to everyone, not just those who can afford promotion.

### Core Principle: No AI-Generated Music

We strictly prohibit AI-composed or AI-generated music. Sonora.Chat is exclusively for human expression, creativity, and emotion. In an era where machines can churn out endless tracks, we're a sanctuary for authentic artistry.

## For Artists: A Free Toolkit to Take Control of Your Career

Sonora.Chat isn't just a listening platform—it's a launchpad for independent musicians. When you join the [Artists Zone](https://artists.sonora.chat), you get:

- **A personal profile and shortlink** (e.g., sonora.chat/yourname) to share with fans.
- **A free mobile app wrapper** that turns your profile into a custom app for your listeners.
- **A personal AI Agent** that comments on your music promotion strategies and generates ideas.
- **Direct donations** via Ko-fi or Buy Me a Coffee with 0% platform commission.
- **Free and unlimited mastering** of your music mixes and tracks.
- **Creative Commons licensing** options to share your work on your terms.

### How Fans Become Promoters

One of our most unique features is the "Hype" system. Fans can promote their favorite artists by hyping them, which improves the artist's search ranking. This puts promotion back where it belongs: in the hands of the community, not corporate algorithms.
