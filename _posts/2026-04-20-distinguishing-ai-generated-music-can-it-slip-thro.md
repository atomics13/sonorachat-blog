## The Telltale Signs: Listening for the Ghost in the Machine

While AI music generation has advanced rapidly, trained human ears can often detect subtle clues. Common markers include **overly perfect, quantized timing** that lacks human feel, **formulaic and predictable song structures** that avoid daring transitions, and **lyrics that are semantically correct but emotionally hollow**. The "uncanny valley" of music often lies in the details: a vocal that never breathes, a guitar solo that's technically flawless but devoid of phrasing, or a chord progression that feels statistically likely rather than emotionally intentional.

However, as models improve, these signs are becoming fainter. The real question isn't just *can we tell?* but *do we care enough to look?* On major streaming platforms, music is often consumed as background noise, pushed by algorithms optimized for engagement, not emotional truth. This environment allows AI-generated content to slip through, valued for its efficiency rather than its artistry.
